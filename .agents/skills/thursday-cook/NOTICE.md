# Source and license notices

Thursday Cook adapts short readbacks and experiment framing from
[Thursday](https://github.com/trythursday/thursday), smallest complete changes and
caller checks from [Ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail), and
bounded context/evidence practices from [pstack](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack).
