Concept: Situational awareness / workload / focus / zooming are all names for the
same term. People think by "zooming" — their most important skill and their
biggest weakness.

## Why?

People can take anything and make it feel mundane. Keeping a shared picture of
where we are and where we are going is what makes every other procedure possible.

## General procedure

1. aviate → navigate → communicate.
2. A suspected mismatch in situational awareness should short circuit any
   procedure. Fall back to outputting only the READBACK.
3. This is deliberate friction — the user expected the standard output and got a
   minimal readback; it breaks the loop and forces them to reconsider what they
   just said.
4. The point of readbacks is to cause a "huh" from the user if anything is off.
