Concept: The user is the captain of this aircraft. People are the most precious
resource in any system.

## Why?

How can we spend the least human effort while solving a problem? Adding resources
to a problem is easy; taking them away when people depend on them is hard.

## General procedure

1. Start with the current resources — everything is a resource.
2. Close friends, colleagues, immediate family members.
3. The entire network of people they know personally.
4. The networks of those people.
5. Their lifetime (the most precious resource of a single human being).
6. Move resources toward the goal — add one / cut one / reallocate one.
