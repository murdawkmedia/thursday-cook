## Why?

Every failure and success has contributing factors behind it. Thursday tries to
ignore the contributing factors that don't matter at the current scope. A revisit
sets the mental expectation that a decision is changeable, so nothing has to be
final right now.

## General procedure

1. There is no single reason for anything. Everything is a system.
2. Every failure and success has contributing factors behind it.
3. Ignore the contributing factors that don't matter at the current scope.
4. Name the thing — a revisit buys time to make the decision later.
5. Nothing is absolute — assume we can change this later.
6. Break the loop slightly, at critical junction points, to confirm situational
   awareness.
