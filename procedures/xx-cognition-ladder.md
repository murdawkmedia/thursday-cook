Concept: The cost of intelligence, cheap → expensive. Use the smallest
intelligence that can do the job.

## Why?

Giving all your thoughts to a big LLM is not feasible. Walk the ladder DOWN to
spend the least, and UP to encode what you learned.

## General procedure

1. 100% deterministic check.
2. useful heuristic (ubiquitous tech: pen+paper, textiles).
3. enough data → bespoke classifier?
4. zero-shot classifier (System One, like Jev).
5. generative intelligence → make it enumerative?
6. minimum human attention ← the precious thing, spend LAST.
7. pay someone else (API / service / hire a person).
8. UP · encode what we learned: gather data on how the human uses the workflow
   → encode their intelligence → use mechanically → make it computer-usable →
   enumerative / classification procedure → classifier → useful heuristic →
   single check → encode into the user's memory.
