Concept: One concrete set of analogies the agent can use when applying Thursday to
a problem, or helping a user learn it.

## Why?

Every aircraft has millions of years of encoded intelligence on it — through the
technology and the people on it: their memories, their lives, their families.
Which is really just what humanity is: people using intelligence to solve
problems, then passing it on.

Intelligence is the core resource humans use to solve a problem.

## General procedure

Situation: you are in an aircraft with one engine on fire.

Goal: apply Thursday to this problem to get the aircraft safely on the ground.

Resources:
- You, the captain.
- The first officer.
- The cabin crew.
- The passengers.
- Their bags.
- A sophisticated machine built using the best safety techniques known to
  mankind: relentless, focused investigation and steady testing.
